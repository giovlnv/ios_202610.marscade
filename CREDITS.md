# Credits

All artwork in Marscade is either drawn in code for this app or comes from free, openly licensed sources.

## Bead Sort pixel pictures
- **Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup / RLTiles 32×32 tiles**, CC0 (public domain dedication).
  Taken from [Snowdrama/CC0-Dungeon-Pack](https://github.com/Snowdrama/CC0-Dungeon-Pack), a repackaging of
  [opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles](https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles).
  Converted to bead pictures with `tools/build_bead_art.py` (cropped, reduced, fewer colors).

## Bead Sort paintings (hard and super hard levels)
Public-domain works, converted with `tools/build_painting_art.py`:
- The Starry Night, Vincent van Gogh, 1889
- The Great Wave off Kanagawa, Katsushika Hokusai, c. 1831
- The Scream, Edvard Munch, 1893
- The Wreck of a Transport Ship, J. M. W. Turner, c. 1810
- Mona Lisa, Leonardo da Vinci, c. 1503
- Rouen Cathedral, Claude Monet, 1894
- Poppy Field near Giverny, Claude Monet, 1885
- Spring in Giverny, Claude Monet, 1890
- The Artist's Garden at Giverny, Claude Monet, 1900

Image files came from the example folders of these GitHub repositories (photographs of public-domain 2D artworks):
[fzliu/style-transfer](https://github.com/fzliu/style-transfer),
[Ekaghni/styletransfer-lite](https://github.com/Ekaghni/styletransfer-lite),
[hwalsuklee/tensorflow-fast-style-transfer](https://github.com/hwalsuklee/tensorflow-fast-style-transfer),
